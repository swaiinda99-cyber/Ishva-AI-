⚡ About Ishva AI

Ishva AI is an autonomous multi-agent operating system created by Ekka Technologies under founder Swai Singh.

How Ishva Works:

Instead of relying on a single generalist model, Ishva uses a Supervisor Agent that decomposes your prompt into atomic tasks, routes them to specialized AI models concurrently (Gemini Flash & Groq LLaMA 3.3), and runs automated OWASP security reviews.

🛡️ Is My Data Safe?

• Zero-Knowledge BYOK: Your API keys stay 100% inside your browser's local memory.
• No Centralized Logging: Your prompts and code are never sold or stored on external analytics servers.
• Direct TLS 1.3 encrypted browser-to-provider connections.
